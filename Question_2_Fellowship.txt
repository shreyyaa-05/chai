Q2. Research Fellowship Knowledge Base

(i) FOL representation of the four policies

1. Every student studies at least one course.
   forall x [Student(x) -> exists y (Course(y) AND Studies(x, y))]

2. A student who studies and passes the same course is qualified.
   forall x forall y [(Student(x) AND Course(y) AND Studies(x, y) AND Passed(x, y)) -> Qualified(x)]

3. A qualified student supervised by a professor is a researcher.
   forall x forall p [(Qualified(x) AND Professor(p) AND Supervises(p, x)) -> Researcher(x)]

4. Every researcher is eligible for the fellowship.
   forall x [Researcher(x) -> Eligible(x)]

(ii) Does the first statement mean everyone studies the same course or that everyone studies AI?

No to both. The statement says that each student studies at least one course. It doesn't say that the course has to be the same for everyone.

For example, the given facts say Studies(Ravi, AI) and Studies(Anu, ML). So we cannot conclude that every student studies AI.

(iii) Can we derive Eligible(Ravi)?

Yes. We can apply the rules one after another.

Step 1: We have Student(Ravi), Course(AI), Studies(Ravi, AI) and Passed(Ravi, AI).
Using policy 2, we get the new fact:
Qualified(Ravi)

Step 2: We have Qualified(Ravi), Professor(DrSharma) and Supervises(DrSharma, Ravi).
Using policy 3, we get:
Researcher(Ravi)

Step 3: Policy 4 says every researcher is eligible.
Since Researcher(Ravi) is true, we get:
Eligible(Ravi)

Hence Eligible(Ravi) can be derived from the given facts and rules.
